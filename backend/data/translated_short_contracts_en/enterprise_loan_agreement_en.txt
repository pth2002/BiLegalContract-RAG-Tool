Enterprise Loan Agreement

Party A (Lender): Investment Co., Ltd.
Party B (Borrower): Startup Technology Co.

1. Loan Amount and Purpose
1. Party A shall provide Party B with a loan of RMB 1,000,000.
2. The purpose of the loan is working capital for business operations. The purpose may not be changed without Party A's prior written consent.

2. Loan Interest and Fees
1. The annual interest rate of the loan is 24%, and compound interest shall be charged monthly.
2. Party B shall pay the interest for the current month on or before the 20th day of each month.
3. If Party B delays the payment of interest, penalty interest shall be charged on the overdue portion at a daily rate of 0.1%.

3. Loan Term and Repayment
1. The loan term shall be 6 months, calculated from the date of disbursement.
2. Principal and interest shall be repaid in a lump sum upon maturity.
3. If Party B makes an early repayment, it shall pay liquidated damages equal to 3% of the outstanding principal.

4. Security Provisions
1. Party B's actual controller and his or her spouse shall provide a joint and several liability guarantee.
2. Party B shall mortgage all of its assets to Party A, and the mortgage registration procedures shall be handled by Party B.
3. The scope of the security shall include principal, interest, penalty interest, liquidated damages, and all expenses incurred by Party A in realizing its creditor's rights.

5. Acceleration
If any of the following circumstances occurs, Party A shall have the right to declare the loan immediately due and payable and require Party B to immediately repay all principal and interest:
1. Party B fails to pay any installment of interest on time;
2. Party B's business condition deteriorates or it becomes involved in major litigation;
3. A change occurs in Party B's actual controller.

6. Liability for Breach
If Party B delays repayment, in addition to paying penalty interest, it shall also bear all expenses paid by Party A to realize its creditor's rights, including attorney's fees, litigation fees, preservation fees, travel expenses, and other related costs.
