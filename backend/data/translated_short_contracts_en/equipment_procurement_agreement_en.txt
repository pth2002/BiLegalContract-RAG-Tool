Equipment Procurement Agreement

Party A (Buyer): Intelligent Manufacturing Plant
Party B (Seller): Imported Equipment Trading Company

1. Subject Matter of Procurement
Party A agrees to purchase one set of automated production line equipment from Party B. The specific specifications and model shall be subject to the product description provided by Party B.

2. Contract Amount and Payment Method
1. Total contract price: RMB 2,000,000 (including freight).
2. Party A shall pay 60% of the total contract amount as a deposit after execution of the contract.
3. After the equipment arrives, Party A shall pay 30%, and the remaining 10% shall be paid after installation, commissioning, and acceptance are completed.
4. If Party A delays payment, Party B shall have the right to postpone delivery and shall not bear liability for breach.

3. Delivery and Acceptance
1. Party B shall deliver the equipment within 90 days after receipt of the deposit.
2. Party A shall complete acceptance within 24 hours after the equipment arrives. If no written objection is raised after the deadline, the equipment shall be deemed accepted.
3. Installation of the equipment shall be the responsibility of Party A, and Party B shall provide telephone technical support.

4. After-Sales Service
Party B shall provide a warranty period of 3 months from the date of acceptance. For faults not caused by human factors during the warranty period, Party B may provide paid repair services.

5. Liability for Breach
1. If Party A refuses to accept the goods without cause or delays payment, it shall pay Party B liquidated damages equal to 20% of the total contract amount.
2. If Party B is unable to deliver on time due to force majeure, Party B shall not bear liability for breach, but shall promptly notify Party A.

6. Force Majeure
Force majeure includes, but is not limited to, earthquakes, typhoons, floods, wars, government actions, shortages of raw materials, delays by suppliers, and other objective circumstances that are unforeseeable, unavoidable, and insurmountable.
