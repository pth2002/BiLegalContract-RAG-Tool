Management Consulting Services Agreement

Party A (Client): Growth Enterprise Co., Ltd.
Party B (Consultant): Excellence Management Consulting Firm

1. Service Content and Objectives
Party B shall provide Party A with consulting services on corporate strategic planning to help Party A improve its management capability and market competitiveness. The specific deliverables shall be determined by mutual consultation based on project progress.

2. Service Fee and Payment
1. The total fee for this project shall be RMB 300,000.
2. Within 3 working days after execution of the contract, Party A shall pay the full amount as an advance payment.
3. If any additional expenses arise after Party B completes the services and such completion is accepted by Party A, the parties shall negotiate them separately.

3. Service Period
The expected service period of this project shall be 6 months, and the specific start and end dates shall be subject to Party B's actual arrangements.

4. Liability for Breach
1. If Party A fails to pay on time, it shall pay Party B liquidated damages equal to 1% of the total contract amount for each day of delay.
2. If Party B fails to complete the services on time, the parties shall resolve the matter through friendly consultation.

5. Dispute Resolution
Any dispute arising from this contract shall first be resolved through consultation. If consultation fails, the dispute shall be submitted to the arbitration commission located where Party B is located, and the arbitral award shall be final.

6. Intellectual Property
The ownership of intellectual property rights in consulting reports, plans, and other work products created by Party B in the course of providing the services shall be separately agreed upon by the parties.

7. Confidentiality
Both parties shall keep confidential the other party's trade secrets learned during their cooperation.

8. Miscellaneous
This contract is executed in two originals, with each party holding one copy, and shall become effective on the date when both parties sign and affix their seals.
