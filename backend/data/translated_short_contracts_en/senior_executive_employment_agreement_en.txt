Senior Executive Employment Agreement

Party A (Employer): Internet Technology Company
Party B (Employee): Zhang Ming

1. Position and Duties
Party A hires Party B as a senior executive of the company, responsible for the company's daily operation and management. The specific duties shall be arranged by Party A according to the company's needs.

2. Compensation and Benefits
1. Party B's annual salary shall be RMB 600,000, paid monthly.
2. Party B shall enjoy all benefits provided under the company's policies.
3. Performance evaluation standards and bonus payments shall be determined by Party A in accordance with company rules.

3. Probation Period
1. The probation period shall be 6 months, and the salary during probation shall be 70% of the regular salary.
2. During the probation period, Party A may terminate the contract at any time without paying any economic compensation.

4. Non-Competition
1. For 5 years after leaving the company, Party B shall not work for any enterprise that competes with Party A, nor engage in a similar business on his own.
2. The non-compete compensation shall be 10% of Party B's monthly salary before departure, paid on a monthly basis.
3. If Party B breaches the non-compete obligation, Party B shall pay Party A liquidated damages of RMB 1,000,000.

5. Termination of Contract
1. Party A shall have the right to adjust Party B's position or terminate the contract at any time according to the company's business needs or Party B's work performance.
2. If Party B terminates the contract, Party B shall give Party A 60 days' prior written notice and shall not leave the position without authorization unless Party A agrees.
3. If Party B leaves without authorization, Party B shall compensate Party A for all losses thereby caused.

6. Intellectual Property
All intellectual property rights created by Party B during employment and within one year after departure that are related to Party B's work shall belong to Party A, and Party B shall cooperate in handling the relevant registration procedures.
