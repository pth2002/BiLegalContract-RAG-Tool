Technology Transfer and License Agreement

Party A (Transferor): Frontier Technology Co., Ltd.
Party B (Transferee): Traditional Manufacturing Enterprise

1. Subject Matter of Transfer
Party A agrees to transfer to Party B the right to use its advanced manufacturing technology. The specific technical scope shall be subject to the list of technical materials provided by Party A.

2. Transfer Fee and Payment Method
1. Entry fee: RMB 500,000, payable within 7 days after execution of the contract.
2. Royalty fee: For products manufactured by Party B using the technology, Party B shall pay Party A a royalty equal to 15% of sales revenue, settled once every quarter.
3. The royalty payment period shall be 10 years from the effective date of the contract.

3. Technical Performance and Acceptance
1. Party A shall provide the relevant technical materials, and Party B shall independently verify the feasibility and effectiveness of the technology.
2. Party A makes no guarantee whatsoever regarding the actual application results of the technology.

4. Contract Term and Termination
1. This contract shall remain valid for 10 years.
2. Party A shall have the right to terminate the contract immediately if Party B fails to pay the fees on time, and the fees already paid shall not be refunded.
3. After termination of the contract, Party B shall immediately cease using the relevant technology, and products already manufactured may not continue to be sold.

5. Confidentiality and Non-Competition
1. Party B shall keep confidential all technical information provided by Party A, and the confidentiality period shall be 5 years after termination of the contract.
2. During the term of the contract and for 3 years after its termination, Party B shall not engage in the same or similar business as Party A, nor cooperate with Party A's competitors.
